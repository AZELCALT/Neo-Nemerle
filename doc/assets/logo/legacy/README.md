# The first Nemerle logo

The revived Nemerle's first logo, kept to remember it by: the current one is `../nemerle.svg`.

`nemerle-source.png` is the drawing it began as. `trace-logo.py` traced it into `nemerle.svg`, a responsive SVG with
three levels of detail (full above 96 px; medium, the disc solid and the notches closed, down to 41 px; the bold N at
40 px and below), and the yellow `nemerle-script.svg` was derived from its purple. The PNGs and the social preview
are its renderings.
